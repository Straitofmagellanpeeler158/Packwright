## 📚 Browse Your Collection

Packwright scans your chosen folder and shows your PS5 dumps and images in a clear list. You can see what is present, open details for each item, and move through your library without digging through nested folders.

Each entry shows the information Packwright could read on its own. If something is incomplete, the app marks it so you can deal with it later. You can sort the list, search for a specific item, and refresh the scan whenever you add new files to the folder.

The point is simple: you should always know what is in your collection and whether it is ready to use.

## ✏️ Fix Missing Details

Dumps often arrive with incomplete information. Titles may be missing, names may be unclear, and some entries may look identical at a glance.

Packwright lets you select an item and fill in the missing details using a plain form. There is no file editing and no manual renaming in your folder. You type what you know, save, and the app updates the entry in your library. This keeps your collection readable and makes it much easier to find the right item later.

If you have many items with the same problem, you can go through them one after another without leaving the app.

## 🔄 Convert and Build Images

Packwright supports the image formats used by PS5 dump collections, including exFAT, FFPKG, FFPFSC, and ZArchive, plus debug packages.

From the image tools, you can:

- Convert a dump or image from one supported format to another.
- Build a fresh exFAT image from a prepared folder.
- Create FFPKG and FFPFSC images from your source files.
- Build ZArchive images for compact storage.
- Work with debug packages alongside your normal images.

Each action shows its progress in the window, so you can see when it is safe to move on. Choose an output folder before you start, and Packwright will write the finished image there instead of overwriting your originals.

## 🩺 Check Library Health

The health check looks through your collection and reports problems in plain language. It can find entries with missing details, images that do not match their expected size, and duplicates that waste space.

Run the health check whenever you add a batch of new files or before you send anything to a console. The report tells you what needs attention and links each problem back to the item in your library, so fixing it is a matter of clicking the item and following the suggestion.

## 📡 Send to a Console on Your Network

Packwright can send supported items to a console on your network. Make sure your computer and console are on the same network, then choose the item you want to send and pick your console from the list.

The app shows the transfer in progress and lets you know when it finishes. If the console is not detected, check that it is powered on and connected to the same network, then refresh the list.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting

**The app will not open.** Close it from the task manager and open it again. If it still will not start, download a fresh copy from the link above.

**My collection is empty after scanning.** Check that you picked the folder that actually contains your dumps and images. If your files are in subfolders, make sure the scan is pointed at the top-level folder.

**Some items show missing details.** That is expected for raw dumps. Select the item and fill in the details using the form.

**The console does not appear in the list.** Confirm that your computer and console are on the same network, then refresh the list.

**An image build fails.** Check that you have enough free space on the output drive and that no file in the source folder is open in another program.
